Elon Musk's first trillion didn't come from Tesla.
It came from rockets.
In June, SpaceX had the biggest stock market debut ever.
It raised $75 billion, and ended day one worth $2.1 trillion.
Musk owned about 43% of SpaceX, but only 13% of Tesla.
That's why, that day, Forbes named him the world's first trillionaire.
SpaceX now makes up nearly four-fifths of his fortune.
But it swings: he has dropped below a trillion and climbed back several times.
This week, Forbes puts him at about $900 billion.
And Tesla's shareholders approved up to another trillion for him, in shares.
But only if Tesla becomes worth $8.5 trillion.
