Apple helped pay for Lionel Messi.
His official MLS pay is $28.3 million a year.
But Inter Miami's owner says he pays Messi $70 million to $80 million a year.
So where does the rest come from?
Apple pays MLS at least $2.5 billion over ten years to stream its games.
When Messi signed in 2023, he got a cut of the money from new subscribers to that stream.
Adidas reportedly offered him a share of its gains too.
Forbes reckons he'll make about $145 million this season.
$75 million of that comes from off the field.
Forbes now counts him as a billionaire.
And his contract runs until 2028.
