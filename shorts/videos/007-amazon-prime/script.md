Amazon charges you $139 before you buy a thing.
That is the yearly price of Prime.
Or $14.99 a month.
It started at $79 back in 2005.
Then $99. Then $119. Then $139.
Prime and Amazon's other subscriptions brought in about $50 billion in 2025.
One bank says members still get ten times their money's worth.
The biggest part is free delivery, about $550 a member last year.
It also reckons another $20 a year would bring Amazon $3 billion.
Meanwhile, Prime Video started showing ads in 2024.
No ads now costs another $4.99 a month.
And those ads feed a business that took $68.6 billion last year.
