Players spent $6.8 billion on Roblox last year.
And Roblox still lost more than a billion dollars.
Partly because a big slice of that money goes straight back out.
In 2025, Roblox paid its game makers $1.5 billion.
Roblox says creators get about 25 cents of each dollar spent in their games.
But the money isn't shared evenly.
The top 1,000 creators averaged $1.3 million each.
That's $1.3 billion of the $1.5 billion.
And one hit can change everything.
Grow a Garden was made by a 16-year-old in a few days.
In June 2025, 21.6 million people played it at once.
By late 2025, 144 million people played Roblox every day.
