Starbucks holds billions of your money, like a bank.
Every time you top up the app or get a gift card, it gets paid first.
In fiscal 2025, that came to about $15.2 billion, counting reward Stars.
Most of it comes back as coffee.
But at the end of that year, Starbucks was still holding $1.75 billion customers hadn't spent.
That's money it has before it makes a single drink.
And some balances are never spent at all.
Starbucks counts those leftovers as revenue. It's called breakage.
Nearly 36 million Americans are active Starbucks Rewards members.
And a third of its US orders are now placed in the app.
