A free game made $9 billion in two years.
That's about $12 million a day.
That was Fortnite, in 2018 and 2019.
Court papers from Epic's fight with Apple revealed it.
Players buy V-Bucks to spend in its Item Shop.
Today, 40% of that shop money goes into a pot for the people who build its islands.
It's shared out by how much people play each island.
By May 2026, creators' islands took 47% of all playing time.
Since 2023, Epic has paid creators more than $1 billion.
But in March 2026, Epic cut more than 1,000 jobs.
Its boss said a Fortnite slump left Epic spending far more than it made.
A week earlier, it had raised the price of V-Bucks, saying Fortnite cost more to run.
Even a free game has to pay its bills.
