McDonald's is a landlord that happens to sell burgers.
It has more than 45,000 restaurants.
But it runs only about one in twenty of them.
About 95% belong to franchisees.
They hire the staff and run the kitchens.
So how does McDonald's get paid?
Twice.
First, a royalty, a cut of every sale.
Last year that came to $6 billion.
Second, rent.
McDonald's usually owns or leases the land and the building.
The franchisee pays rent to use them, month after month.
Last year rent came to $10.4 billion.
That is far more than the royalties.
Selling burgers is the franchisee's job.
Collecting rent is McDonald's job.
In its main markets it owns about 56% of the land and 80% of the buildings.
