McDonald's earns more from rent than from royalties.
It does not run most of its restaurants.
About 95% belong to franchisees.
They run the kitchens and hire the staff.
McDonald's usually owns or leases the land and the building.
So franchisees pay it rent, plus a royalty on their sales.
Last year rent came to $10.4 billion.
Royalties came to $6 billion.
