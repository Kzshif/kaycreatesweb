A Switch 2 now costs $50 more.
Blame the AI boom.
In September, Nintendo raised the US price to $499.99.
AI data centres are buying up memory chips, and their price has soared.
Nintendo expects higher parts costs and tariffs to cost it about 100 billion yen this year.
That's roughly $640 million.
Then sales fell.
Last quarter it sold 2.67 million Switch 2 consoles, down 41% on a year before.
Still, it has sold more than 26 million since launch.
That's faster than the first Switch, or the PS5.
Nintendo still expects to sell 16.5 million this financial year.
