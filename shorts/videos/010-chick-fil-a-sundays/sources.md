# Sources: 010 Chick-fil-A

How these were checked: this session cannot open company, regulator or news
sites directly (network policy). Every figure below is therefore
**search-verified**: a web search returned the quoted wording from the page at
the exact link given. "Agrees" names a second, independent search result that
gives the same figure. Anything marked below "high" confidence is listed at the
top of the batch report for a check by hand before upload.

Chick-fil-A is private. Its sales figures come from its franchise disclosure
document as reported by the trade press, and QSR's per-restaurant average for
it is QSR's own estimate. Publication dates given for the 2026 QSR Magazine
articles are approximate (the year is right; the day was not shown in search).
