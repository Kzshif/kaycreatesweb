Chick-fil-A takes every Sunday off.
And its restaurants still out-sell McDonald's.
The average Chick-fil-A took about $7.7 million in 2025.
The average McDonald's took about $4.1 million.
Almost double, in six days instead of seven.
There are only about 3,300 Chick-fil-As, against 13,700 McDonald's.
Yet it is America's third-biggest fast-food chain, with $23.9 billion in sales.
Analysts once reckoned Sundays cost it $1.2 billion a year.
Its boss says that won't change.
Running one costs an operator just $10,000 up front.
Back in 2018, fewer than 1% of applicants got picked.
But Chick-fil-A owns the restaurant.
And it takes 15% of sales, plus half the profit.
