Google paid Apple $20 billion in a single year.
Not for phones.
For one setting.
Search in Safari and the answers come from Google.
It is the default, already set when you buy the phone.
Google pays Apple to keep it that way.
In 2022 the bill reached $20 billion.
The number came out in court.
Under the deal, Apple got 36% of the ad money from those searches.
