Google paid Apple about $20 billion in one year.
Not for phones.
Not for chips.
For one setting.
Type a question into Safari on an iPhone.
The answers come from Google.
Not Bing, not anyone else.
That is the default, set before you open the box.
Every search can show ads, and Google earns when people click them.
So Google pays Apple to keep that setting.
Apple keeps choosing Google.
In 2022 the bill came to about $20 billion.
Apple's cut was 36% of the ad money from Safari searches.
That figure slipped out in court.
It was meant to stay secret.
Google's lawyer was seen to wince.
Defaults stick.
That habit is what Google is paying for.
And Apple gets paid for a choice you never made.
