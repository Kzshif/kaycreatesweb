This can has cost 99 cents since 1992.
The price is printed right on it.
Even tariffs haven't moved it.
They made AriZona's cans about 40% more expensive.
That's about $40 million extra, and the founder is swallowing it.
If the price followed his costs, a can would be $1.99.
So how does AriZona still make money?
Founder Don Vultaggio says nothing solves problems like volume.
The cans got thinner, and the lines run faster.
The can even shrank, from 23 ounces to 22.
And he has hardly spent a dollar on advertising.
He says traditional advertising is dead.
Forbes puts his family's fortune at about $5 billion.
He calls the price his little way of giving back.
