Costco's membership fees equal half its profit.
Last year its warehouses sold $270 billion of goods.
But buying and selling them cost almost as much.
Its low prices leave little profit.
The money comes from the card.
Shoppers paid $5.3 billion in fees just to walk in.
Since 2024 a basic US card has cost $65 a year.
Those fees add up to about half of what Costco earned from running the business.
