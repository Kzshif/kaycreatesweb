Costco barely makes money on what it sells.
Last year its warehouses sold $270 billion of goods.
But buying those goods and running the stores cost almost as much.
The low prices are the point.
Each basket leaves Costco only a sliver.
So where does the money come from?
The card in your wallet.
Shoppers paid $5.3 billion in fees just to walk in.
That money arrives before a single item is sold.
There is nothing to stock, ship or store.
Those fees equal about half of what Costco earned from running the business.
And people keep paying.
In the US and Canada, 92% of members renewed.
Since 2024, a basic US card has cost $65 a year.
The hot dog and drink still cost $1.50, as they did in the 1980s.
Cheap food keeps you coming back.
