# Sources: 021 Minecraft

How these were checked: this session cannot open company, regulator or news
sites directly (network policy). Every figure below is therefore
**search-verified**: a web search returned the quoted wording from the page at
the exact link given. "Agrees" names a second, independent search result that
gives the same figure. Anything marked below "high" confidence is listed at the
top of the batch report for a check by hand before upload.

Persson's share of the sale is a press estimate before tax (70% of $2.5
billion). Copies sold are not the same as players: some people buy the game on
more than one device. The film's box office is the cinemas' takings, shared
between the cinemas and the studios that made it; it is not money Microsoft
received. Quotes marked "(paraphrase of the search result)" give the result's
wording where the page itself was not shown.
