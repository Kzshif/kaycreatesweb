Minecraft beat Beyoncé to this $70 million house.
In 2014, Microsoft bought Minecraft's maker, Mojang, for $2.5 billion.
Its creator, Markus Persson, owned 70% of it.
His share was worth about $1.75 billion.
That December, he outbid Beyoncé and Jay-Z for a Beverly Hills mansion.
He paid $70 million, a Beverly Hills record.
And Microsoft? Minecraft kept growing.
Back then, it had sold about 54 million copies.
Today it has sold more than 425 million.
By 2021, add-ons from outside creators had brought in over $350 million.
And the Minecraft movie made nearly $1 billion worldwide.
Now Persson is selling the mansion.
The asking price? The same $70 million.
