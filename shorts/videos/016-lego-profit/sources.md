# Sources: 016 Lego

How these were checked: this session cannot open company, regulator or news
sites directly (network policy). Every figure below is therefore
**search-verified**: a web search returned the quoted wording from the page at
the exact link given. "Agrees" names a second, independent search result that
gives the same figure. Anything marked below "high" confidence is listed at the
top of the batch report for a check by hand before upload.

Lego reports in Danish kroner; the dollar figures are CNBC's conversions.
Hasbro's 2025 operating result was a loss of $943 million on its reported
figures, because of a one-off goodwill write-down; this video uses Hasbro's
adjusted operating profit of $1.14 billion, which flatters the comparison in
Hasbro's favour, so "twice" is if anything an understatement. The Lego
ownership page is undated (the date given is that of the 2025 results).
