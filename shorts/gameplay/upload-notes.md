# GTA6ISOVERRATED: upload notes

Five shorts (23-28 seconds), cut from your playthrough recording, each read by a different voice.
Each opens with one second of picture and music before the voice and subtitles start, and carries
the GTA6ISOVERRATED watermark. Post one a day, in order. Each folder's `out/` has the video, a
`captions.srt` to upload as English subtitles and a `thumbnail.png` (1080x1920).

Thumbnails: if YouTube offers a custom thumbnail when you upload the Short, use `thumbnail.png`.
If it only lets you pick a frame, pick one from the first seconds (the hook is on screen). The same
image works as the cover on TikTok and Instagram Reels.

At upload, every video:

- Tick "Altered or synthetic content": the narrator is a synthetic voice.
- Not made for kids.
- Pin the comment below, then reply to the first comments in the first hour. Arguments in the
  comments are what push a short, so answer the angry ones too (stay on the game, never on people).
- Hashtags go at the end of the description.

## Facts used (keep them accurate if you edit)

- GTA 5 launched in September 2013; GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date.
- GTA 6 was delayed twice: from fall 2025 to 26 May 2026, then to 19 November 2026.
- Standard Edition $79.99; Ultimate Edition $99.99, which adds bonus vehicles, weapons, outfits and in-game shops the standard edition does not get. The boxed copy is a download code, with no disc. Most big new games are $69.99.
- Metascore of 99 out of 100 at launch. Launch-week player complaints include long drives, long conversations, chores, toned-down chaos and falling through the map.
- GTA: Vice City came out in 2002. Leonida, GTA 6's state, is based on Florida.
- The first GTA 6 trailer passed 90 million YouTube views in 24 hours (December 2023).

The opinions ("overrated", "mid", "all hype", "a remix", "a marketing campaign", "a very expensive
driving simulator") are the channel's take, said as opinions. Keep the criticism on the game, not on
