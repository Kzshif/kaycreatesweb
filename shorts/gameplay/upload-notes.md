## Facts used (keep them accurate if you edit)

- GTA 5 launched September 2013; GTA 6 launched 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date.

- Metascore 99 at launch; launch-week player complaints include long drives, long conversations, chores, toned-down chaos and falling through the map.
- GTA: Vice City came out in 2002.
- The first GTA 6 trailer passed 90 million YouTube views in 24 hours (December 2023).
