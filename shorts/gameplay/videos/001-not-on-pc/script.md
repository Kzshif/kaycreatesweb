GTA 6 fans won't admit this, so I will.
Here are three reasons it's the most overhyped game ever.
One. Thirteen years after GTA 5, and Rockstar still delayed it. Twice.
Two. It costs $80, and the boxed copy doesn't even have a disc.
It's a download code. In a box.
Three. It's not even on PC. No date. Nothing.
Thirteen years of hype, for this?
Overrated. Change my mind. You can't.
