GTA 6 isn't a game. It's a marketing campaign.
Its first trailer got over 90 million views in a day.
Then it got delayed. And delayed again.
Thirteen years of hype.
And hype sells games. But hype doesn't play the game. You do.
And after all that waiting, you're still just driving around a fake Florida.
Is GTA 6 actually fun? Or did the hype just win?
